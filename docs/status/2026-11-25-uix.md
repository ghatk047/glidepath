# Status 2026-11-25 · UIX: desktop fit and the first-time viewer's tour (UIX-01, UIX-02)

Branch `bl/UIX-01-desktop-density`, stacked on `claude/project-thread-nhdg5u` (WIZ, PR #2). No tier-2 change, no contract change. Items: `docs/backlog/UIX.md`.

## What changed
- **Density layer** (`ui/src/glass/styles.ts`): above the tablet breakpoint the token gap, panel padding, panel radius, hero and xl sizes step down; again on screens up to 900 px tall. Tablet and phone keep the token values.
- **Top bar**: title, scenarios and tools on one row at 1280 and wider; "Find any number" shows icon and ⌘K up to 1600 px (label kept as accessible name, tooltip added).
- **Baseline**: line items scroll inside their panel under a sticky header, one line per row, full text in a tooltip.
- **Levers**: list scrolls inside its panel; display numbers capped so the three columns fit 1280 (no more sideways scroll); confidence chip readable (was accent ink on glass).
- **Wizard**: step 1 levers in two columns; step 3 timing one line per lever, scrolling in its panel.
- **VPs, Path, Cockpit**: gap chip stays in its card, rows tighter, gauge smaller on short screens, bridge capped at 36vh; histogram "Bar" label flips left at the right edge.
- **Tour** (UIX-02): every step has an `about` line saying what the screen is for; `TOUR_VERSION` 2.
